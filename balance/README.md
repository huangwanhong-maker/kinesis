# Balance Laws on Graphs

Status: **draft** (2026-11-16). Module `kinesis::balance` ([decision 0005](../development/decisions/0005-balance-laws-on-graphs.md)).

## 1. Concepts

A field here is a density on the cells of a graph: people per km² on a grid of a city, a concentration on the compartments of a network. A balance law says how it changes: by what crosses the faces between cells, and by what is made or lost within each. In finite volumes the flux across a face is a sum of diffusion (down the gradient), crowding (a diffusivity that grows with the density, the porous-medium equation) and drift (carried up a potential), each conservative by construction; what is made is growth (linear, logistic, Gompertz's), a constant or a covariate. Every law is linear in its coefficients.

A record of the field is a few snapshots, perhaps five or ten years apart, of thousands of cells: far too few times for the derivatives that induction's templates need, and far too many components for its declarations. Two estimates suit it. The integral form integrates the law between snapshots by the trapezoid, so that each cell and each interval gives one linear equation in the coefficients; shooting runs the law from the first snapshot and fits the run to the rest by Levenberg–Marquardt, with no bias of the step. A diffusion found negative (the backward heat equation, ill-posed) is set aside and said to be. A forecast is judged on snapshots kept aside against carrying the last one forward, as a whole and as a pattern, once its total is set to the record's: where the quantity went, apart from how much there was. Beside the laws stand a rate of its own for each cell, and the record's dynamic modes by the method of snapshots, which works with a dozen snapshots of thousands of cells.

This is what the theory note's §11 listed as a discretised fluid, done for balance laws of a density on a graph rather than through the grammar: the components are indexed by the graph, and the law is a sum of operators on it. Theory: [docs/theory.md](../docs/theory.md) §8.1.

## 2. Content

- `graph.hpp`: `Edge{a, b, conductance}`, `Graph{volume, edges}`; `Grid` and `grid(rows, cols, mask, spacing)`, a square grid's kept cells joined to their four neighbours; `laplacian`, `crowding` (Δ(u²/2)), `drift` (−∇·(u∇Φ), centred), `content` (Σ V u by Kahan's sum); `Currents` and `currents(grid, flux)`, the vector at each cell from its faces' fluxes.
- `law.hpp`: `Kind` (constant, linear, quadratic, gompertz, diffusion, crowding, drift, source), `Term`, `Law{terms, coefficients}`, `symbol`, `name`, `kind_of`; `evaluate`, `rate` (with an extra rate per cell, a source), `flux` (each edge's, in units of the quantity per unit time), `stiffness` (Gershgorin's bound); `RunOptions`, `Run{t, u, clipped, steps}`, `simulate`: Runge–Kutta of the fourth order with the step held within its stability, densities made negative set to zero and the quantity so added reported.
- `learn.hpp`: `Record{t, u}`, `first`; `LearnOptions{weights, floor, threshold, keep, most, well_posed}`; `Estimate{law, standard_errors, residual, explained, rows, terms, iterations, converged, method, dropped}`; `integral(graph, terms, record, covariates, options)`, plain or thresholded on scaled columns (induction's stlsq); `shooting(graph, start, record, covariates, options)`, projected so that no diffusion turns negative; `Score{t, predicted, rmse, persistence_rmse, skill, pattern_skill, total_error, change_correlation}`, `score`, `forecast`; `OwnRates` and `own_rates`, the baseline of a rate per cell.
- `modes.hpp`: `Mode{eigenvalue, rate, amplitude, shape, size}`, `Modes{t0, step, singular_values, rank, modes, residual, at(t)}`, `modes(record, rank, tolerance)`: exact DMD by the method of snapshots, eigenvectors of the reduced operator by inverse iteration, amplitudes by least squares.
- `balance.hpp`: the whole module.

*Caveat.* The standard errors are those of least squares and treat the equations as independent; neighbouring cells and successive intervals are not, so they understate. The drift is centred, not monotone where it outruns the diffusion across a cell; a run says what it clipped. A law with few shared coefficients can describe only what it holds: a city's density is pinned by its buildings, and a diffusion that would flatten its centre within a year is not found in its record (the plug-in's experiment, in the infrastructure).

## 3. Checks

31, each against a closed form or a law the check declares and recovers: the five-point Laplacian zero on a constant and exactly 4 on x² + y²; diffusion, crowding and drift each keeping the content to rounding; the rate's moving part equal to minus the divergence of the edges' fluxes; a drift up an eastward potential carrying the current east; one cell's logistic and Gompertz growth against their solutions to 10⁻⁹; a diffusion keeping its content over a run; a drift that outruns its diffusion clipped, the content rising by exactly what was clipped; Fisher's front on a strip running at 2√(rD) less Bramson's delay; growth, ceiling, spread and drift recovered from a record taken often by the integral form, and from five snapshots by shooting where the integral form is biased; Fisher's three terms kept, and no other, among the whole library by thresholding; the truth within three of shooting's standard errors under 2% noise; a sharpening record's negative diffusivity set aside; a forecast with the right pattern and a fifth too many having all the pattern's skill; persistence with no skill and the truth with all; a rate per cell reproducing exponential growth; from ten snapshots of eighty cells moved by a linear law of four modes, its rank, its eigenvalues to 10⁻¹³, a forecast four steps on and a residual of rounding; uneven times refused.

## 4. Dependencies

`kinesis::induction` (for stlsq, and through it `kinesis::kernel`); calx's `linalg` (rectangular matrices, QR and least squares, the normal inverse, the symmetric eigenproblem, eigenvalues). The checks use calx's `random` and `verification`.
