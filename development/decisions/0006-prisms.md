# 0006. Prisms: What a Record of Positions Identifies of a Path Under a Bound on Speed

- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally
- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Decided by:** the user ("integrate the evolutionary reasoning, discovery and interpretation mechanism into the law domain … and has an evidence spacetime visualizer"; "we are in fact building a multi-modal archive with well-formed ontologies, for recognizing the trajectory generativity of a system") and Claude; the infrastructure's ADR 0127

## 1. Context

Theories of a case place people at events, and the evidence places them elsewhere at other times. Whether a person could have been at a door between being seen at an inn and answering a telephone at home is a question about the set of paths a record of positions admits under a bound on speed: Hägerstrand's space-time prism (1970), formalised by Lenntorp (1976) and Miller (2005). It is what a record identifies (the module braids' question for planar trajectories, the kernel's invariant 5), it names no object in nature, and nothing in the library or its siblings answered it. physis holds causal sets and exact spacetimes; tekmerion's `located` holds the share of a disc within a disc; neither holds paths between fixes.

## 2. Decision

1. **A module `prisms`** on the kernel and calx's Brent minimiser. A record is fixes (a place within a radius at an instant) and a bound on speed.
2. **The deepest point of finitely many discs**, min over p of max (|p − c_i| − r_i), exactly: the minimiser is a point where one, two or three discs are active, and every such candidate is evaluated.
3. **Consistency**: necessary pairwise conditions over every pair of fixes (the pair that breaks named, short by so much); a witness history by cyclic projections onto the discs and the bounds on each step, checked exactly; otherwise undecided. With exact fixes the consecutive pairs suffice.
4. **The slice at an instant**: the bracketing fixes' cones (exact for exact fixes, an outer bound otherwise).
5. **Reach and presence**: on each piece between fixes the depth of the slice and a region is convex in time, so its least value is found by Brent's method and the instants within reach form one interval a piece; presence in a region during an interval is the set {in, out} over admissible histories: "in" impossible when out of reach (an alibi, certified), possible with a witness; "out" impossible when a fix's disc lies within the region, admissible with a witness that stays out; whatever is not decided is said.
6. **Euclidean and stated.** Paths are straight; obstacles and networks are not seen. A record's frame and units are the caller's.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **In physis**, beside causal sets: the prism is the Alexandrov interval of a cone structure of speed v, but what is asked of it here is identification from a record, which is this library's.
- **In tekmerion**, beside `located`: what a record of positions admits is not evidence and serves dynamics as much as law; tekmerion uses it.
- **Exact feasibility with radii by propagating convex sets** (each step a Minkowski sum and an intersection): exact, but its sets are no longer intersections of discs. The witness with an exact check, and an honest "undecided", serve until a case needs more.
- **Sampling time for reach**: the convexity makes Brent's method sufficient and its tolerance statable.

## 4. Consequences

- 32 checks: the deepest point against closed forms and a fine grid on 200 random sets; consistency of a walk at and below its speed; three discs that meet pairwise and not together never said to be consistent; presence in closed form (a place out of reach short by 45 m at the middle instant; a window from 245 s to 355 s; a fix that makes presence necessary); a cyclist's window past a door in closed form, and the door out of reach at a lower speed. kinesis's 496 in all.
- tekmerion's `scenarios` (its decision 0009) uses the module for every person's path in an account, and the law domain's `theories` for whether a person could have been at the scene under the readings of its open terms.
- **Not built:** obstacles and networks (a path along streets, a river crossed at a bridge); time-uncertain fixes as such (a span is widened into the radius by the caller); joint presence of several movers at one point.
