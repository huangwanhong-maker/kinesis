# 2026-11-13

## kinesis Founded

kinesis is a library for dynamical systems as objects of study and of inference ([decision 0002](../decisions/0002-scope-boundaries-and-dependencies.md), [decision 0003](../decisions/0003-the-first-modules.md), [decision 0004](../decisions/0004-averages-coupling-by-an-action-and-a-cascade.md)). It stands on calx, morphe, physis and tyche.

- **One language for every kind of system.** A system is declared by its change, its structure, its constraints and what is told apart. The same notation declares a flow, a map, a system with events, a stochastic differential, a system given by its action, an open quantum system, a grammar, and a system made of systems. A script that is wrong is told where: the line, the column, and what may follow there.
- **A law need not be an equation.** A system given by its action is varied, exactly, into the equations of its stationary paths, and the result is a declaration again. Every expression is a term with exact derivatives.
- **What a declared law does.** Runs with events; stochastic paths in Itô's or Stratonovich's calculus; the words of a grammar with the histories of each; the stationary state of an open quantum system. Over a long time: Lyapunov's exponents with the error of their finite time, equilibria, bifurcation diagrams, basins with each end named, landscapes, signs of slowing down, and the averages of what a system observes with the law by which they scale.
- **What a record identifies.** A braid read from sampled trajectories is certified only under a stated bound on speed, with the margin by which it holds; otherwise the windings the record admits are listed. An answer from data is a set of values with its assumptions, never one value given as if it were sure.
- **Laws from records.** A declaration with unknowns is a template, and a law is fitted, discovered or matched by filling them: unknowns inside an action among them, sparse discovery with no law proposed, candidates ranked on a part of the record kept aside. What is found is a declaration again.
- **Systems joined.** Flows merge into one declaration exactly. Parts given by actions are joined by an interaction action, and the whole keeps its energy. Parts of different kinds, a flow with a map or with noise, are advanced side by side.
- **A catalogue of thirty models** from many fields, each with its source, among them a shell model of turbulence whose exact balances are held. A record is matched against the catalogue in every order of its components.

It is held to 433 checks against closed forms, published values, exact solutions and laws the checks declare and then recover.

What the language reads and the library does not yet run is said where it is asked: fields weighted by an action, the reversal of a stochastic differential, unknown functions.
