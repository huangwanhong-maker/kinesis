# 2026-11-18

## Prisms: Where a Mover Could Have Been

A new module, `prisms` ([decision 0006](../decisions/0006-prisms.md)), answers what a record of positions says about a path when the mover cannot go faster than a stated speed: Hägerstrand's space-time prism, for the infrastructure's theories of the case ([ADR 0127](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/Noetarium-The-Science-and-Humanity-Infrastructure/blob/main/development/decisions/0127-theories-of-the-case-in-space-and-time.md)).

- **Whether a record can be travelled:** yes, with a path through every fix that is checked; no, with the two fixes no path can join and how far short it falls; or not decided, said so.
- **Where the mover could be at an instant,** and when a place could be reached: one window between each pair of fixes, found exactly.
- **Presence in a place during a time,** over every path the record allows: an alibi is certified, a possibility comes with a path through the place, and what is not decided is said.
- **Checks.** 32, held to closed forms and a fine grid; kinesis has 496.
