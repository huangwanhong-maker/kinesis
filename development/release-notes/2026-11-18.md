# 2026-11-18

## Prisms: Where a Mover Could Have Been

A new module, `prisms` ([decision 0006](../decisions/0006-prisms.md)), answers what a record of positions says about a path when the mover cannot go faster than a stated speed: Hägerstrand's space-time prism, for the infrastructure's theories of the case ([ADR 0127](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/Noetarium-The-Science-and-Humanity-Infrastructure/blob/main/development/decisions/0127-theories-of-the-case-in-space-and-time.md)).

- **Whether a record can be travelled:** yes, with a path through every fix that is checked; no, with the two fixes no path can join and how far short it falls; or not decided, said so.
- **Where the mover could be at an instant,** and when a place could be reached: one window between each pair of fixes, found exactly.
- **Presence in a place during a time,** over every path the record allows: an alibi is certified, a possibility comes with a path through the place, and what is not decided is said.
- **Checks.** 32, held to closed forms and a fine grid; kinesis has 496.

## Systems Across Scales

A new module, `scales` ([decision 0007](../decisions/0007-systems-across-scales.md)), declares systems whose quantities live on a hierarchy of units, blocks within districts within a city, for the infrastructure's cities across scales ([ADR 0128](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/Noetarium-The-Science-and-Humanity-Infrastructure/blob/main/development/decisions/0128-indexes-and-cities-across-scales.md)).

- **Levels that agree.** Quantities cross levels only by named operators: sums and reductions up, a parent's value down, shares, logits and z-scores among siblings, the graph Laplacian, and the inflow of a spatial interaction model. What is allocated down sums to what was there, and a ratio is the ratio of sums at every level.
- **Each law at its own pace:** flows over their own steps, maps once a period, fast quantities held at rest; laws stepped yearly run beside laws stepped monthly.
- **Nothing made up.** Every input says how it is known (observed, estimated, declared, modelled), and a model refuses to run with what it lacks named, level by level.
- **What the declarations do:** runs over draws of every declared range and estimated error, and the declarations ranked by what they move.
- **Fits at any level,** with the unknowns a record does not identify said to be, and the fit judged on years held aside against standing still.
- **Checks.** 66, held to closed forms (allocations, the orders of the schemes, Harris and Wilson's equilibria, exact aggregation); kinesis has 562.
