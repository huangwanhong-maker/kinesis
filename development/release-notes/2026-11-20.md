# 2026-11-20

## Critical Transitions in Structured States

A new module, `transitions` ([decision 0008](../decisions/0008-critical-transitions-in-structured-states.md)), asks whether a record shows a system losing its stability. It is honest about what it cannot see, for the infrastructure's detectors of critical transitions ([ADR 0135](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/Noetarium-The-Science-and-Humanity-Infrastructure/blob/main/development/decisions/0135-detectors-of-critical-transitions.md)).

- **Any kind of state.** A record may hold:
  - a quantity, or a field over units;
  - compositions such as a population by age, read in log-ratios with each unit's size kept apart;
  - distributions given by histograms, read by their quantiles;
  - covariance matrices, read by their logarithm against a reference.

  Units may have neighbours and sit in a hierarchy. Every level of the hierarchy is then read in its groups' totals and in the contrasts within them.
- **Every indicator says what it sees and what it is blind to.** Slowing down precedes local bifurcations under slow forcing. It does not precede tipping by noise, by the rate of a change, through a crisis or across a threshold; nor a critical mode the noise does not excite, or one that shows only in the units' mean.
- **Every test says how often it is wrong at the record's length.** The change from the first window to the last is tested against the stationary process fitted to the record. Running the same pipeline under a declared slowing gives the test's detection rate, false-alarm rate and ROC area. Further nulls and a grid of windows and bandwidths check that a result survives the choices behind it.
- **Three verdicts:**
  - *signal*;
  - *no signal*, said only where the test was powered and the change is small under the slowing it was tested against;
  - *undecidable*, with every reason. Among the reasons: a record already near a unit root, or a red noise whose own rate does not confirm the slowing.
- **More:**
  - the restoring rate estimated directly, four ways (one under red noise, which reports the pair of rates it identifies);
  - where the dominant eigenvalue goes (+1, −1 or the circle);
  - where the slowing shows and what excites it;
  - wells and switching;
  - a likelihood test that gives a tipping time only when its interval is bounded.
- **Checks.** 77, held to closed forms and to test models with known answers, positive and negative; kinesis has 639.
