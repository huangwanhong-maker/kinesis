# Release Notes: 21 November 2026

## Densities Moved by a Generator, and Learned Laws That Say What They Cannot Support

A density on a grid of cells can now be moved by drift and diffusion that vary from cell to cell, in types between which it switches ([decision 0009](../decisions/0009-densities-and-learned-laws-as-evaluators.md)): run by implicit steps that keep its mass, brought to its stationary density, and changed by interventions (a transfer to those below a line) composed in either order, so that the exact effect of order under a declared law can be read. A value equation of a stock drawn on in switching regimes is solved by the upwind scheme, and its policy is the drift of the density, whose forward equation is the value equation's generator transposed.

A law learned from a record can now be held to the forecasts that need no law, and is told when a baseline beats it; a record's support is kept, and an intervention that goes where the record never went is flagged at its step; an ensemble of laws gives the claim that one sequence of interventions is better than another a standing (holds, fails, arguable, undecided); bagged sparse fits say how often each term is kept.
